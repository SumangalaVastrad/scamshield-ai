# Fake job offer and task scam

## Short description
Scammers reach out via WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS offering lucrative part-time work-from-home jobs such as liking YouTube videos, rating Google Maps locations, or reviewing hotels. After paying small initial bonuses, they demand hefty 'prepaid task deposits' which you cannot withdraw.

## How it usually looks
"Hello! Earn Rs 3,000 to Rs 8,000 daily from home. Simple part-time task: just like 3 YouTube videos and get paid Rs 150 immediately. Contact our HR manager on Telegram @EarnDaily_HR."

## Red flags
- Extremely high payouts for trivial tasks (liking videos, reviewing products).
- Communication moved quickly to encrypted chat apps like Telegram.
- Initial small payouts (Rs 100 - 500) to gain trust.
- Demand for 'security deposits', 'VIP task fees', or 'tax clearance' to unlock your accumulated earnings.

## What to do now (not yet paid or shared anything)
1. Delete the message and block the sender on WhatsApp/Telegram.
2. Legitimate employers never ask candidates to pay money or recharge accounts for a job.
3. Report the phone number or Telegram channel as scam/fraud.

## What not to do
- Never transfer any money, deposit, or registration fee for a job opportunity.
- Never share bank account details or PAN card with unverified contacts.

## What to do if already clicked, shared, or paid (Urgent)
1. Stop sending any further money immediately, regardless of what the scammer claims about unlocking your funds.
2. Note down all recipient bank accounts, UPI IDs, and transaction IDs.
3. Call the national cybercrime helpline immediately to register a complaint.
4. Notify your bank about the fraudulent fund transfers.
