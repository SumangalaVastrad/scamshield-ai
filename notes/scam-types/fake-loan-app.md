# Fake loan app harassment

## Short description
Fraudulent digital lending apps offer instant loans with no credit check. Once installed, they siphon contacts and private photos from the user's phone, disburse a small amount after high deductions, and harass the victim and their contacts with abusive calls and morphed photos.

## How it usually looks
"Instant Cash Loan: Get Rs 50,000 in your bank account in 5 minutes! No CIBIL score required. Install our quick loan app now: http://bit.ly/fast-rupee-loan"

## Red flags
- Loan disbursed without proper KYC or regulated NBFC underwriting.
- App requires invasive permissions: contacts, media/gallery, camera, and call logs.
- Repayment period is absurdly short (typically 6-7 days) with 40-50% deductions as processing fees.
- Threatening messages, abusive voice notes, or extortion using contacts list.

## What to do now (not yet paid or shared anything)
1. Verify if the lending entity is registered with the RBI or partnered with an approved NBFC.
2. Never download loan apps from random links, social media ads, or third-party websites.
3. Deny contacts and photo storage permissions to unnecessary applications.

## What not to do
- Never pay extortion demands or blackmail fees—scammers will keep demanding more.
- Never install APK files from untrusted third-party links.

## What to do if already clicked, shared, or paid (Urgent)
1. Uninstall the malicious app immediately and revoke device permissions.
2. Inform close contacts and family that your phone data was breached and to ignore abusive messages.
3. Lodge a formal complaint with the local police cyber cell and the national cybercrime portal.
4. Report the app link on Google Play / security portals.
