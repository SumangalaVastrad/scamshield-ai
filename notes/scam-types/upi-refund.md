# UPI refund or collect request

## Short description
Fraudsters send a UPI 'Collect Request' or ask you to scan a QR code claiming they are sending you money, a cashback, or an OLX sale payment. In reality, entering your UPI PIN always debits money from your account.

## How it usually looks
"Sir, I have sent you Rs 5,000 for your OLX item. Just open your Google Pay / PhonePe, click approve, and enter your UPI PIN to claim the money into your bank."

## Red flags
- The golden rule of UPI: You NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
- Fraudster insists you scan a QR code to 'credit' your account.
- Receiving an unexpected 'Collect Request' pop-up on BHIM, PhonePe, Paytm, or Google Pay.
- Aggressive pressure over phone call urging you to tap and confirm right away.

## What to do now (not yet paid or shared anything)
1. Reject the collect request immediately on your UPI app.
2. Remember: Receiving money never requires a PIN, password, or biometric scan.
3. Block the sender's UPI ID and phone number.
4. Report the fraudulent collect request directly in your UPI app.

## What not to do
- Never enter your 4-digit or 6-digit UPI PIN when expecting to receive money.
- Never scan QR codes sent by buyers on OLX, Facebook Marketplace, or WhatsApp.

## What to do if already clicked, shared, or paid (Urgent)
1. Call your bank helpline immediately and block UPI services and net banking.
2. File an urgent complaint on the national cybercrime portal or helpline with the UTR number.
3. Take screenshots of the UPI transaction details, recipient UPI ID, and chat logs.
