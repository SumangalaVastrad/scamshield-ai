# Fake KYC update

## Short description
Fraudsters send SMS or WhatsApp messages claiming your bank account, SIM card, or wallet KYC is expired or suspended. They pressure you to click a link or call a number immediately to avoid account blocking.

## How it usually looks
"Dear Customer, your SBI account KYC is suspended. Your account will be blocked today within 24 hours. Click here to verify your PAN and Aadhaar: http://bit.ly/sbi-kyc-update"

## Red flags
- Creates extreme urgency ("blocked today", "suspended immediately").
- Includes a web link (especially shortened or non-bank domains).
- Demands sensitive credentials (account number, PIN, OTP, PAN).
- Banks never send links via SMS asking you to complete KYC.

## What to do now (not yet paid or shared anything)
1. Do not click the link or reply to the message.
2. Verify your account status by logging into your official bank mobile app or visiting your nearest branch.
3. Report the fraudulent SMS to your telecom operator and the national cyber reporting channels.
4. Block and report the sender.

## What not to do
- Never enter your internet banking credentials, debit card details, PIN, or OTP on links received via SMS or chat.
- Never call the phone number provided in the SMS.

## What to do if already clicked, shared, or paid (Urgent)
1. Call your bank immediately using the number on your debit card to freeze online banking and block your debit card.
2. Report the incident immediately to the national cybercrime helpline or portal.
3. Save screenshots of the message, link, and transaction IDs for reference.
