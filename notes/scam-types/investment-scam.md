# Investment and stock tip group scam

## Short description
Victims are lured into WhatsApp or Telegram groups offering insider stock tips, IPO allocations, forex, or cryptocurrency trading. Scammers display fake high-profit dashboards and demand investments transferred into multiple individual bank accounts, then freeze withdrawals.

## How it usually looks
"Exclusive Institutional VIP Trading Group: Get 100% guaranteed returns on upper-circuit stock calls and pre-IPO allotments. Download our proprietary trading app and double your capital in 15 days."

## Red flags
- Promises of guaranteed, risk-free high profits (real markets never have guaranteed returns).
- Money is deposited into diverse individual savings accounts or shell entities, not registered brokers.
- Unofficial app downloaded via APK link rather than Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
- Demands 20%-30% advance "tax" or "handling charges" when you request a withdrawal.

## What to do now (not yet paid or shared anything)
1. Verify if the entity or advisor is registered on the official SEBI website (sebi.gov.in).
2. Exit and report any unsolicited WhatsApp or Telegram trading group.
3. Only trade through recognized, registered stockbrokers and depositories.

## What not to do
- Never transfer investment money to personal savings accounts or UPI IDs.
- Never install APK files sent on messaging platforms.

## What to do if already clicked, shared, or paid (Urgent)
1. Refuse to pay any withdrawal fees, taxes, or unlock charges—these are further extortion tricks.
2. Report the fraud immediately to the national cybercrime portal/helpline.
3. Inform your bank to report the beneficiary accounts and attempt inter-bank freeze.
4. Preserve all chat histories, payment slips, and website screenshots.
