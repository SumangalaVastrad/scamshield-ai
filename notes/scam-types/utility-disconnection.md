# Utility disconnection notice

## Short description
Scammers send alarming messages claiming your electricity or gas connection will be disconnected tonight because last month's bill was not updated. They provide an individual mobile number to call to prevent the disconnection.

## How it usually looks
"Dear Consumer, your electricity power will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM from the electricity office because your previous month bill was not updated. Please immediately contact our electricity officer at 9876543210."

## Red flags
- Sent from a regular 10-digit mobile number rather than a verified government or utility header (e.g., BESCOM, TNEB, BSES).
- Threatens abrupt power cut in a few hours without prior formal postal or account-level notice.
- Asks you to call a mobile number or install a third-party app to pay a nominal Rs 10 charge.

## What to do now (not yet paid or shared anything)
1. Check your real electricity bill and payment status through your regular official utility app or portal.
2. Ignore the threat and do not call the mobile number.
3. Report the phone number on cybercrime or telecom fraud reporting portals.

## What not to do
- Never pay bills to individual mobile numbers or unofficial links.
- Never install any remote desktop or APK file suggested by the 'officer'.

## What to do if already clicked, shared, or paid (Urgent)
1. Call your bank immediately if you shared card details or installed a remote access app.
2. Disconnect your phone from Wi-Fi and mobile data to prevent unauthorized remote operations.
3. Lodge an official complaint with the national cybercrime helpline.
