# Lottery and prize scam

## Short description
Victims receive WhatsApp voice notes, letters, or SMS claiming their mobile number has won a grand lottery (like KBC 25 Lakh lottery) or luxury car. To release the prize, scammers demand upfront payments for government tax, processing fees, or currency conversion.

## How it usually looks
"Congratulations! Your WhatsApp number has won Rs 25,00,000 in KBC Lucky Draw 2026. To claim your lottery amount, contact Manager Rana Pratap on WhatsApp at +91-9876543210 and deposit Rs 12,500 tax fees."

## Red flags
- You won a contest or lottery that you never entered or bought tickets for.
- Legitimate lotteries never ask winners to pay advance fees or taxes into individual bank accounts.
- Audio recordings with sound effects or forged identity cards of public figures.

## What to do now (not yet paid or shared anything)
1. Delete the message and block the sender.
2. Remember that no genuine organization requires you to pay money to receive a prize.
3. Warn family members about these recurring lottery tricks.

## What not to do
- Never send any money as 'registration fees' or 'tax deposit'.
- Never share your Aadhaar, PAN, or bank account statements.

## What to do if already clicked, shared, or paid (Urgent)
1. Stop all further payments immediately—the scammers will invent more fees endlessly.
2. Note the beneficiary accounts and UPI IDs used for fund transfers.
3. Call the national cybercrime helpline or visit your local police station to file an FIR.
