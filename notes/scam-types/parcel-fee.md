# Fake parcel or customs fee

## Short description
Scammers impersonate courier services like India Post, BlueDart, DTDC, or customs officials claiming your package is held due to an incorrect address or unpaid fee. They demand a small fee through a link, which steals your card information.

## How it usually looks
"India Post: Your package IND983421 cannot be delivered due to incomplete address. Please update your address and pay redelivery fee of Rs. 25 at: http://cutt.ly/indiapost-redelivery or your parcel will be returned."

## Red flags
- Claims your parcel is stuck, even when you didn't order or expect any shipment.
- Asks for a nominal fee (Rs 10 to Rs 50) as bait to capture card numbers and CVV.
- Domain name in the link is not the official courier domain (e.g., `indiapost-update.xyz`).
- Sense of urgency warning that package will be destroyed or returned in 24 hours.

## What to do now (not yet paid or shared anything)
1. Do not click the link or make any payment.
2. Check the tracking number directly on the official courier portal (e.g. indiapost.gov.in) typed manually.
3. If you have no pending shipments, delete and ignore the message.
4. Report the phishing sender to telecom or cyber platforms.

## What not to do
- Never pay delivery fees via links sent through SMS or WhatsApp.
- Never enter card numbers, expiry dates, or CVV on non-official websites.

## What to do if already clicked, shared, or paid (Urgent)
1. Immediately contact your bank to block your debit/credit card and dispute unauthorized transactions.
2. If an OTP was entered or money debited, file an emergency complaint via the cybercrime helpline.
3. Keep the SMS and payment transaction ID as evidence.
